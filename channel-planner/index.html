Zigbee / Wi-Fi / Thread Channel Planner

Zigbee, Thread and Wi-Fi all share the 2.4 GHz band, and Wi-Fi is roughly a thousand times louder. Tell it which Wi-Fi channels are in the air around you and it works out where your mesh should sit. Runs entirely offline — nothing is sent anywhere.

In the full version

This free version gives you the recommendation and the spectrum picture, which is the part most people need. The paid version adds the bits you want when the answer is not clean:

  • Every one of the sixteen channels scored, each with the reason — which Wi-Fi network it collides with, whether it lands on a Bluetooth advertising channel, whether it is in the microwave band.
  • Step-by-step instructions for changing channel in ZHA, Zigbee2MQTT, deCONZ and Thread, including what will need re-pairing.
  • A separate Thread channel chosen so it does not fight your Zigbee mesh.
  • A printable one-page plan, and copy-as-text.
  • What to do instead when no channel scores well — which is the real answer in a block of flats.
Frequencies from the IEEE 802.15.4 and 802.11 channel plans. Scoring is a planning model, not a measurement — it cannot see the baby monitor in the next room. Verify with your coordinator's own link-quality figures after you move.
